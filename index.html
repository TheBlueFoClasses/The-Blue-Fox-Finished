At The Blue Fox, we believe that creating art shouldn't be stressful, and that anyone can be an artist as long as you believe in yourself!

Whether you're picking up a paintbrush for the first time in years or refining your unique artistic style, our welcoming classes are designed for you. With expert guidance, proven teaching methods, a supportive atmosphere, and a vibrant creative community, it's easy to see why students return again and again. The joy of creating and becoming your best self is the real journey.

Meet the Instructor

Meet your lead instructor of art, Aryn Lill Diemand! An award-winning muralist and fine artist, Aryn has been sparking creativity in students since 2005. Whether she’s painting public art across Minnesota or teaching her signature curriculum through The Blue Fox, Aryn’s goal is to help every student—“big or small”—find their unique style.

With degrees in Studio Arts and Fashion Design, Aryn brings a world of creative experience to every class. She spent years honing her teaching craft in California’s top art programs before returning home to MN to launch her own traveling studio. Join her for a class to see why her students love her!

"Create something that comes from your soul and share it with the world." — ARYN

Our Studio Philosophy

Why our classes are one of a kind.

Fun

Splatter paint, try something new, share stories, and meet new friends. A Blue Fox class is all about having fun and making memories while learning new techniques.

Inviting

Slow down, enjoy the ride, and feel at home. Our inviting spaces are chosen to make you feel comfortable and welcome, whatever your age or skill level.

Technical

We are passionate about helping every student grow. Our curriculum emphasizes the fundamental elements of art with proven application methods to guide your process.

Creative

Discover your unique visual voice. We encourage customization, self-expression, experimentation, and completely free styling.
